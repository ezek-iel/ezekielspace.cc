I don't like writing motivational essays. Yet, this is going to be the first one I'll ever write. Why? because its needed.

> This letter was from a journal entry (it was meant to be confidential)

## There's always a guy

- There's a guy that knows 5 programming languages
    
- There's a lady that has a lot of connections in the fintech industry
    
- There's a guy using the latest iPad 2025 his company gave him
    
- There's a lady who won ₦35 million last week. _She might be set for life_.
    

## But what about me?

- Me that I've just started, am I playing with my life or what?
    
- These guys are hardworking and lucky. Me, I'm trying to coast through
    
- Am I not in the same Computer Science? Why do I feel everyone is ahead and I'm behind.
    

## Let's agree on one thing

We both agree that until now. We didn't put in much effort into your self, skill and craft.

Also, that we did not do the necessary work required to get the output expected.

**For all this, I have one reply**

## The end justifies the means, not the beginning

I am sure you're still a student, starting out life (the real stuff) or about to graduate (_between ages 19 - 23_).

This might sound cliché. There is more than enough time (_right now_) to figure out where you want to go and put in the necessary effort required to get there.

Sometimes, even after the effort, the results might not seem forthcoming. It might not even make sense. **Trust me, It didn't for me**.

> _I failed 3 interviews in 3 weeks from 3 different companies around 9am on the 9th month of 2025_

So yeah, it won't be easy for some of us.

In these scenarios, still remember, **the end justifies the means, not the beginning.**  
Yet, we are just starting.

## Lastly

Time counts, if you are a bit retrospective (_with the little time you've spent on this planet_)

Every little hour you waste (scrolling, gist-ing, FIFA, Sporty) everyday counts. And worse, it compounds.

Guys, you see doing stuff spend their time wisely.  
Notice it.  
If you choose to, emulate it.

**Sounds cliché?, I know**

## The Bible says,

> Then the LORD answered me: “Write down this vision
> 
> and clearly inscribe it on tablets, so that a herald may run with it. For the vision awaits an appointed time;
> 
> it testifies of the end and does not lie. Though it lingers, wait for it,
> 
> since it will surely come and will not delay

I don't know when it will come, it might be early, it might be late.

All I know is that it will surely come, before the end (_As long as I run with it_).

And, I am just starting.
