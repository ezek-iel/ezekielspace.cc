Within the last 3 weeks or so, I just realized that everybody knows a lot.
  
No, not cumulatively, individually.

It makes sense, [my generation is the most exposed to information amongst every other generation](https://www.collegenp.com/article/why-gen-z-is-the-smartest-generation).

With AI, information is even way more reachable than before.  

Yet, with all these, I keep asking myself a question; Why are we more unfulfilled, or at least; Why do we feel this way?


Let's start with the productivity shtick. Even the closest John or Jane beside you knows the latest productivity tips and tricks to 10x your output in 0.1x the time.

Yet, [we are actually more lazy than previous generations](https://www.newsweek.com/gen-z-says-theyre-laziest-generation-1915450#:~:text=If%20you%20ask%20Gen%20Z%2C%20the%20rumors%20are,laziest%2C%20including%2050%20percent%20of%20Gen%20Zers%20themselves.).

If you don't still believe me, Have you wondered why we enjoy not being stressed out, Even after the whole rhetoric of *"Hard work = Soft life"* has been flogged into our brains.

Or how most of us randomly has a business idea but only a tiny percentage of us actually execute them.

Everybody knows the latest agentic harness but hasn't even tried out any agentic engineering practice yet.

## There has to be a reason

It could be Analysis Paralysis, Information Fatigue, Illusion of Competence.

I can't really tell what the issue is, or if there is even one.

I won't even diagnose a fix.

I just want you to be aware of it.

You're welcome 😊
