We are told to read books. Readers are leaders. But don't read too much - you can only retain less than 1% of what you read so just read summaries.  No, if I were you, I will read the whole book as it helps you discover relationships between main points and also the intent of the author

But remember only we face the consequences of our actions, so live with your deathbed as the focus. And also remember the family members beside you on that bed.

Don't have a girlfriend too early, build your life first. You need someone to be beside you while you're building your life. No, girlfriends are a distraction. They can also be a fantastic partner while you figure things out.

Software engineering is dead. AI is coming for our jobs. You're wrong, AI is messing up in the workplace. No, AI is only coming for those who do not understand the fundamentals. Learn the fundamentals. AI is better than you for the fundamentals. Learn how to orchestrate agents. Though its better to know how to build your own.

Save your money. No, Your money will depreciate if you save, Invest. Investing makes money go up, but forex makes money go up, up and crypto, up, up, up. But be careful, you can lose a lot of money on crypto. Only if you don't use my algorithm that you need to join my telegram channel to access.

Oh, you need to be disciplined to achieve success. No, your brain runs on a "Willpower battery", just "time-box" around it. No, thats BS, its your environment causing the indiscipline. Walk in the forest for 3hrs and see how you will change. BS.... , all BS, all you need is my Notion template that I paywalled all of a sudden. I use it to achieve 20x more as a meta engineer.
