Emails are an essential part of a business operating system.

Yet in 2026, emails are still incredibly hard to organize. We as human beings, drown in an influx of leads, clients, advertisements and noise.

I want to build for this. Its called **Vale**.

Vale makes emails more productive and organized. It does this by enabling customers to create intelligent workflows. These workflows kickoff immediately when a mail enters your inbox. Imagine;

- Intelligent automatic replies to product suppliers.
- Automatic forwarding of organization's core values to new hires.
- Customer complaints automatically logged and sent to the customer service department.
- Intelligent search that pulls relevant context from your mails and create brief summaries.

## But, Here's the truth: **I haven't built it yet**

I am starting this project from scratch and I'm going to document the journey at vale.ezekielspace.cc.

I want to build a tool that actually solves people's inbox nightmares.

If you're tired of fighting your inbox and want to help shape a more human way to work, follow along. **Let's build it together**.
