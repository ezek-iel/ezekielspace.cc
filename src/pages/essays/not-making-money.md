I think as a species and as a profession we have entered this stage where everything we do is for the ultimate purpose of making money.

I got into this tarpit of really scrutinizing ideas in my head based on whether they could be monetizable or not.

Even friends ask me if I am working on a new AI start-up, with someone on a project or just making money in general.

My reply is always __No__, I don't see myself on any of that __for now__.

I really enjoy building software and writing code to actually solve problems. The idea of making money (_for me_) is really stripping that joy out of it.

I believe that __for now__, enjoying what I love to do is very important now that I am in the early stages of my life.

So for the next year or so, I will be focusing on one thing; __Enjoying programming as it is (_no capitalism attached_)__. 

This will include; building projects without libraries (_to understand underlying concepts_), participating in hackathons and doing weekly dev-logs and tutorials.

I will also be sharing my progress with you guys.
